At Green Toys, sustainability isn’t just a part of what we do—it’s who we are.

We believe that great toys should do more than spark imagination—they should also help build a betterworld. Since our founding, we have led the way in sustainable toy manufacturing, proving that high-quality,eco-friendly playthings can be made responsibly.

Our Values

  • Sustainability
    First

    From the very beginning, Green Toys has been a leader in eco-friendly toy manufacturing. We use 100% recycled materials and continue to push the boundaries of sustainable production. Every choice we make is rooted in reducing waste, conserving resources, and creating a healthier planet for future generations.

  • Uncompromising Quality & Safety

    No matter where our toys are made, they meet the highest standards of safety and durability. Free from BPA, phthalates, and harmful coatings, Green Toys are rigorously tested to ensure they are as safe as they are fun. Parents can trust that every Green Toy is built to last through years of play.

  • Ethical & Responsible Manufacturing

    Our commitment to ethical production remains at the core of our brand. Whether made in the USA or in carefully chosen facilities abroad, we partner with manufacturers who share our values, ensuring fair labor practices, environmentally responsible processes, and the same high-quality craftsmanship Green Toys is known for.

  • Innovation &
    Evolution

    As pioneers in the recycled toy industry, we are constantly evolving to make the best sustainable toys possible. Expanding our production allows us to continue innovating while maintaining our dedication to quality and environmental responsibility. Change is part of growth, and Green Toys remains committed to leading the way in eco-conscious play.

Our Story

At Green Toys, we believe that play can change the world. Since day one, our mission has been to create toys that are safe, sustainable, and built for endless play—while doing right by the planet and future generations.
