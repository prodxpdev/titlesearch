features:
  - title: Honest availability
    details: Registry records from RDAP and WHOIS, confirmed by registrars. A name with no registry record is "Not registered", never "Available", until a registrar says so.
  - title: Who's there, and do they compete
    details: For taken names, Titlesearch reads the site, spots parked and for-sale pages, captures a preview, and judges market overlap with your description.
  - title: Names from your description
    details: Besides variations of names you have, it can suggest new ones from what you're building, and check them in the same search.
  - title: Read-only by construction
    details: It can't register, renew, transfer, or change any domain or DNS record. Site text is treated as untrusted data, never instructions.
